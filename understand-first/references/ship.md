# Ship phase

## Purpose
Make a scoped, evidence-backed GO/NO-GO assessment of the proposed delivery.
Ship is a decision record, not deployment permission.

## Inputs
- Build evidence against the current acceptance behavior.
- Exact intended delivery scope, exclusions, residual risks, and recovery or handoff information.
- Missing earlier phases are acceptable; use supplied requirements, code, and evidence and label assumptions.

## Deliverable
Record concise cards in the current phase source and same-page workbook HTML:
- **Decision:** GO or NO-GO, with the evidence that supports it.
- **Delivery scope:** what this assessment covers and what it excludes.
- **Residual risks:** known impact, unknowns, and the boundary of confidence.
- **Recovery and handoff:** the scoped recovery path and information the next owner needs.
Keep claims evidence-backed; identify blockers instead of manufacturing certainty. This reference is guidance, not a standalone document or generated checklist.

## Completion
The reader has an explicit GO/NO-GO decision, exact scope, evidence, unknowns, residual risks, and handoff or recovery boundary.
Progress still needs explicit chat approval. GO is an assessment, never deployment or project approval.

## Boundary
Do not infer GO from missing evidence, authorize deployment, add scope, or demand a full-workflow restart.
Ask only about blockers and keep the assessment scoped to the proposed delivery.
